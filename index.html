PURPOSE

What it's built to do

  • Track daily expenses
  • Monitor monthly budgets
  • Visualize spending patterns
  • Receive automatic budget alerts
  • Simplify financial management

BACKGROUND

Why it exists

Managing daily expenses by hand is slow, and most expense apps are hard to use, cluttered with ads, missing automatic logging, or weak on visualizing spending.

Nova Tracker combines Flutter, Cloud Firestore, Firebase Authentication, and Firebase Cloud Messaging into one modern, intelligent tracking experience.
